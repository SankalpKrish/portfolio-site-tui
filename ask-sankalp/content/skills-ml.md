I work hands-on with PyTorch, TensorFlow and Librosa, and I have used them on real problems
rather than only tutorials.

The concrete instance is MIDI.ai, where I use a Hybrid Transformer Demucs v4 for stem separation,
Spotify Basic Pitch for note transcription, and YAMNet for timbre classification. Choosing those
three means knowing what each model is actually good at and putting the right one on the right
stage, which I think is more the skill than any single model's architecture.

I would describe myself as strong on applied audio and pipeline work and still developing on
training models from scratch. I have used pre-trained models intelligently; I have not published
a novel architecture.

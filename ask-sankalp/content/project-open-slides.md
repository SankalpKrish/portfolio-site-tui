Open Slides is an end-to-end AI presentation platform, and it is the largest thing I have built
on my own. I started it in March 2026 and I am still working on it.

The idea is that you talk to it. A chat-driven agent builds, edits, and exports slide decks,
documents, sheets, and websites in real time. Not a generator that hands you a file at the end —
an agent loop you stay in conversation with while the artefact changes underneath you.

It is deployed on Vercel. I have also written a `pdf-server` that runs on a VPS, which is what
handles the heavier export work.

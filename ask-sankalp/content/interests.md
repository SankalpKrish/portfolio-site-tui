Vehicle spotting is the big one — planes, trains, cars, bikes, ships. If it moves, I am watching.
It is a genuinely analytical hobby, not just a passive one; you are reading liveries, spotting
units, and reasoning about how a system is meant to be used.

I also research random topics, which I would characterise as falling into Wikipedia spirals and
losing several hours before noticing.

Beyond that: philosophy, where I am opinionated enough that people tell me not to argue about it,
and food, where I am not apologising about it.

These are not filler. Spotting in particular is why I like interfaces that expose their internals
— a terminal that shows you the tool calls underneath is more fun to me than one that hides them.
That is not why I built this site, but it is not a coincidence either.

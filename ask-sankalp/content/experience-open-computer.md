Since May 2026 I have worked as a Member of Technical Staff (intern) at Open Computer, in
Bengaluru. It is a four-person team.

I own frontend and design development for the company's LLM-based agent product. Concretely
that means I build the design system the product is rendered with, and I designed and set up the
workflow that defines how the agent exercises curiosity and discoverability — that is, how it
decides what to go and look at next rather than only answering what it was asked.

The work is on deliverables slated for the public beta launch, so most of the specifics are
under trade-secret protection and I cannot go into them. I can talk about the shape of the work
and the problems it poses.

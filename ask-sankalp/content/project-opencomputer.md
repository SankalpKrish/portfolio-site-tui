Open Computer is the company I intern at, and the product is a platform for discovering AI
skills and plugins. The idea is that there is a rapidly growing supply of agent capabilities and
no good way to find the one you want, so the product is about curiosity and discoverability.

I have been on this since May 2026, working on the frontend and design. I have written about my
specific responsibilities separately, because the interesting part is not the product description
but what I actually own.

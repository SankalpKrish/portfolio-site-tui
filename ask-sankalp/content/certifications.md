I completed two Google professional certificates through Coursera, both in June 2026.

The Google Project Management Professional Certificate covered project lifecycle management, risk
mitigation, budgeting, and scope and stakeholder management.

The Google AI Essentials certificate covered prompt engineering, generative AI for business, data
insights, and workflow automation.

They are professional certificates rather than degrees, and I would rather overstate what they
are not than understate what they are: they are evidence that I have formalised the product and
AI fundamentals rather than picking them up only by doing.

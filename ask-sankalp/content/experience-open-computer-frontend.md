At Open Computer I do three things that are unusual together for one person.

First, frontend and design development for an LLM-based agent product. I ship the product UI
end to end, not just components of it.

Second, I built the design system around an ASCII-based image design language. The reason for
going ASCII rather than pixels is cross-context compatibility and performance: the same visual
vocabulary survives being rendered in a terminal, in a chat bubble, in a log, and on a slow
connection, without redrawing anything.

Third, I designed the workflow that governs how the agent exercises curiosity and
discoverability. That is the part of agent design that is not about the model at all — it is
about what the agent is allowed to go look at, and when.

I am studying a B.Tech in Digital Transformation (Computer Science) at Atria University in
Bengaluru. I started in August 2024 and graduate in August 2028, so I am in my second year.

The degree deliberately mixes computer science with product and business. My coursework covers
data structures and algorithms, relational databases and SQL, full-stack web development on
both frontend and backend, supervised and unsupervised machine learning, and deep learning. On
the product side it covers design thinking, agile sprint methodology, AI-driven problem solving,
and financial modeling.

That combination is why my projects tend to be end-to-end. The product coursework is the part
that pushes me to finish and ship something rather than leaving it working in a notebook.
